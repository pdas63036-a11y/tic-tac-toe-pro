📘 Game Rules

  • Board: The game is played on a 3×3 grid.
  • Turns: Two players take turns placing marks (X and O).
  • Start: Player X usually starts first.
  • Objective: Be the first to form a straight line of 3 marks (row, column, or diagonal).
  • Draw: If all 9 cells are filled and no player forms a line, the game is a draw.

🎮 How to Play

  1. Enter player names (optional).
  2. Select game mode: 2 Player or AI.
  3. Click an empty cell to place your mark.
  4. Turns alternate automatically between players.
  5. The game detects wins or draws automatically and shows the result.

✨ Features

  • Player Name Customization: Rename Player X and Player O in the controls.
  • AI Opponent: Play against the computer; AI moves automatically after your turn.
  • Winning Line Animation: A glowing line is drawn over the three winning cells.
  • Scoreboard: Winners get +1 point; scores persist until you reset them.
  • Sound Effects: Move, win, and draw sounds. Use the sound toggle to mute/unmute.

⚙️ Controls

  • Restart Game — resets the board but keeps the score.
  • Reset Score — clears all saved scores.
  • Mode Toggle — switch between 2-player and AI.
  • Sound Toggle — turn game sounds ON/OFF.
